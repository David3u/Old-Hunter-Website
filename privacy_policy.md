# Privacy Policy for *Old Hunter Website* Extension

_Last updated: August 25, 2025_

## Overview
The **Old Hunter Website** Chrome extension is designed solely to improve navigation on the Hunter Schools portal (`hunterschools.myschoolapp.com`) by automatically redirecting pages and enabling quick access to assignment information.  

We respect your privacy. This extension does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data Collection
- The extension does **not** collect personally identifiable information.  
- No browsing history, cookies, or credentials are collected.  
- No data is transmitted to third parties or external servers controlled by the developer.  

## Permissions Justification
The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

- **Tabs**: Used to detect when a Hunter Schools tab is updated and redirect it if necessary.  
- **Scripting**: Used to inject a small function (`dueButton`) into the Hunter Schools page to automatically click the "Due" filter.  
- **Host Permission (`https://hunterschools.myschoolapp.com/*`)**: Required to fetch assignment data from the official Hunter Schools API.  

These permissions are limited to the Hunter Schools domain and are not used elsewhere.

## Data Sharing
The extension does not share data with any third parties. All requests to `hunterschools.myschoolapp.com` are direct between your browser and the school’s servers.

## Changes to This Policy
If this extension’s functionality changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the new version will be made available in the Chrome Web Store listing.
